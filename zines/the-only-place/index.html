The Only Place That Felt Like Home

Mt. Hood, Biggie Smalls, and the place that felt like home.

Around 2018 to late 2020, the Mt. Hood cabin was the only place that ever felt like home. Nature and isolation helped Spencer find himself; psychedelic experiments sparked a creative path. This journal gathers that time into photographs and a personal narrative, with Biggie Smalls close by.
